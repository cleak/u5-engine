This macOS build is NOT signed or notarized
===========================================

The project did not have an Apple Developer ID certificate available when this
release was built, so macOS Gatekeeper will refuse to open the app with a
message that it "cannot be opened" or "is damaged".

To open it anyway:

1. Move "U5 Engine.app" to your Applications folder.
2. Remove the download quarantine flag in Terminal:

       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/U5 Engine.app"

3. Open the app normally.

On macOS 14 and earlier you can instead Control-click the app, choose Open,
and confirm. On macOS 15 and later, try to open it once, then go to System
Settings > Privacy & Security and choose "Open Anyway".

Only do this for a download you got from the project's GitHub releases page,
and compare its checksum with SHA256SUMS:

    shasum -a 256 u5-engine-*-macos-universal.zip
